## Cardiac Segmentation Report

### Clinical Information
The patient is a 62-year-old female with a history of arterial hypertension and a positive ECG (ST+). She has a history of tobacco use (3 packs). She is overweight but not diabetic, and has no family history of coronary artery disease. Cardiac biomarkers show a Troponin level of 5.2 and NT-proBNP of 2989. The left ventricular ejection fraction (FEVG) is 47.

#### Clinical Interpretation

**Cardiac Status:**
The patient presents with elevated cardiac biomarkers (Troponin 5.2, NT-proBNP 2989), suggesting myocardial injury or ventricular strain. The Left Ventricular Ejection Fraction (FEVG) of 47% indicates mildly reduced systolic function, which is a key finding in assessing cardiac function.

**Structural Assessment:**
The segmentation analysis reveals a total myocardium mass of 78.29 g. Importantly, the analysis indicates a small area of myocardial infarction (2.59 mL) and no evidence of no-reflow phenomenon (0.0% of myocardium). The fraction of myocardium infarcted is low at 3.47%.

**Risk Factors:**
The patient has significant cardiovascular risk factors, including age, female sex, arterial hypertension, and a history of tobacco use.

**Infarction Status:**
The presence of a small, quantifiable area of myocardial infarction (2.59 mL) requires further correlation with clinical presentation and ECG findings. While the percentage of infarcted myocardium is low, the absolute volume needs clinical context. The overall findings suggest evidence of myocardial injury, consistent with the elevated troponin level. Further clinical correlation with the imaging findings and serial monitoring of cardiac biomarkers is recommended.
